Frequently asked questions

How do I verify my account?

Enter your UT Tyler email address, then enter the six-digit verification code within 10 minutes. In a local development environment, the code may appear in the server console instead.

How do I reserve and collect an item?

Make an offer, agree on a meeting in a public place and confirm the exchange only after handover. Reservations without handover confirmation can expire.

What if a listing or message is suspicious?

Use the report and block controls. If there is a problem with an exchange, select the dispute option in Transactions. See community safety guidance.

Can UniMarket refund my purchase?

UniMarket does not process payments or guarantee refunds. Submit a dispute or support ticket for assistance. Do not share payment credentials.

Why was my listing rejected?

Check My Listings for duplicates. You may publish up to 10 new listings per hour. Duplicate listings submitted within 10 minutes may be rejected.

What if my account is suspended?

Contact the UniMarket project administrator through the established project contact channel. A suspended account cannot sign in to submit tickets.

Contact support

Only you and authorised moderators can view your support tickets. Do not include passwords, verification codes or payment details.
