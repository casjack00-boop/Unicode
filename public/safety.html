Community safety

Report

Reports are private to authorised project operators. A report does not automatically suspend an account.

The reported message will be included as evidence.

Block student

Prevents new messages and offers between both accounts. Existing conversations and reservations remain accessible. You can unblock a student in Settings.

Safer exchanges

Meet in a public, well-lit location. Inspect the item before paying. Keep communication in UniMarket and do not share passwords or financial credentials.

UniMarket does not handle payments, guarantee refunds or provide emergency assistance. If you face immediate danger, contact emergency services.
